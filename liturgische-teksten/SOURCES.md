# Sources

Documents consulted for the translations of the Canon of Supplication
(Paraklesis) to the Theotokos in this directory, and for the pitch-mark
reconstruction.

## Texts used

- **`kanon-ro.typ`** — Romanian text, Archdiocese of Râmnic:
  <https://arhiepiscopiaramnicului.ro/paraclise/paraclisul-preasfintei-nascatoare-de-dumnezeu>
- **`kanon-en.typ`** — English text, Antiochian Orthodox Christian
  Archdiocese of North America, *The Paraklesis Service with the Little
  Supplicatory Canon* (asterisk-pointed):
  <https://antiochianprodsa.blob.core.windows.net/liturgicalinstructions/Paraklesis%20Little%20SERVICE.pdf>

## Psaltic (Byzantine neume) scores

- **Stavropoleos Monastery, Bucharest — Small Paraclis**
  (*Primul Paraclis al Preasfintei Născătoarei de Dumnezeu*), in neumes,
  as sung at Stavropoleos. Only the irmoi of Odes 8 and 9 are notated in
  full. The Ode 8 irmos, *Pe Împăratul ceresc…*, starts at the bottom of
  p. 17 and continues on p. 18; the Ode 9 irmos, *Mântuiți prin tine
  fiind…*, is on p. 19. See `NEUMES.md` for how they were turned into
  pitch marks:
  <https://www.stavropoleos.ro/wp-content/uploads/Psaltica/Rugaciuni/paraclis_primul.pdf>
- **Stavropoleos Monastery — Great Paraclis**
  (*Paraclisul Preasfintei Născătoarei de Dumnezeu*, set to notes by
  Deacon Cornel Constantin Coman). Not the canon used here, since its
  Ode 1 irmos is *Pe Cel ce se purta în car…*:
  <https://www.stavropoleos.ro/wp-content/uploads/Psaltica/Rugaciuni/paraclis_mare.pdf>
- **Byzantion (Romanian psaltic publisher)** — contents of their paid
  Paraclis edition (Odes in plagal glas IV = glas 8):
  <https://byzantion.ro/wp-json/wp/v2/posts/93>,
  shop: <http://shop.byzantion.ro/product/38/paraclisul-maicii-domnului.html>
